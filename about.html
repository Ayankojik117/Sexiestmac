About Us

A community built one step at a time

MaC Foundation started with a simple idea: you don't need permission to start making a change, you just need to take the first step.

Our belief

Small steps, real change

We believe meaningful change begins with small steps. Not grand declarations — a cleanup drive, a mentoring session, a workshop, a conversation. MaC Foundation exists to turn that belief into consistent, organized action across the issues our community cares about most.

Our approach

Led by young people, for the community

Every project at MaC is designed and run by our own volunteers. That's deliberate — it means the people closest to a problem get to build the solution, and they leave with real leadership, project management, and advocacy experience along the way.

How we work

From idea to impact

Identify

A volunteer team spots a need in one of our five focus areas — social welfare, sports & mental wellness, environment, women empowerment, or education.

Design

The team scopes a project: goals, timeline, who it serves, and what it needs — real project design and management, done by the volunteers themselves.

Run

The project launches on the ground, with MaC volunteers leading outreach, logistics, content, and delivery from start to finish.

Reflect & grow

Every project closes with a review — what worked, what the community needs next, and what each volunteer takes forward into the next one.
